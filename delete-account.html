Request Account Deletion — PushDuel

You can request deletion of your PushDuel account and all associated data at any time, whether or not you still have the app installed.

How to request deletion

Email us from the address associated with your PushDuel account, with the subject line "Delete my PushDuel account", and we'll process your request.

Request account deletion

What gets deleted

Once your request is verified, we delete:

  • Your account and sign-in credentials
  • Your profile (display name, country, profile picture, gender/push-up style setting)
  • Your gameplay history, rankings, streaks, and achievements
  • Your victory clip, if you recorded one
  • Your friends list and any pending friend requests or challenges

How long it takes

We aim to complete deletion requests within 30 days of verifying the request came from the account owner.
