PushDuel Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 September 2026

PushDuel ("we", "our", or "the app") is a mobile app for camera-tracked push-up practice, ranked duels, and optional daily app-unlock goals. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and the choices available to you.

Information we collect

  • Account and profile information: your email address, unique public username and tag, optional country, optional profile picture, and records of your minimum-age and legal-policy confirmations. Sign-in may use email and password, Google Sign-In, or Sign in with Apple on supported Apple devices. Names supplied by Google or Apple are not published to other players.
  • Gameplay information: push-up counts, match results, ratings, rankings, streaks, achievements, friends, challenges, blocks, and reports.
  • Notification information: a private device push token used to deliver challenge, friend, and match notifications.
  • Analytics information: Firebase Analytics collects app-instance and device information, app usage, screen views, session activity, and whether solo or offline challenges finish. Our custom analytics events do not contain account IDs, email addresses, opponent names, or camera footage. We use this information to understand usage and improve the app.
  • Diagnostic information: limited technical error, crash, app-version, and device-platform details used to investigate failures. Diagnostic messages are scrubbed to reduce accidental inclusion of email addresses, links, or tokens.

Camera and photos

Live camera frames used for rep tracking are processed entirely on your device and are not uploaded, transmitted, or stored. An on-device machine-learning model uses the live feed only to estimate body position and count push-ups. If you choose a profile picture, the selected image is uploaded to cloud storage. PushDuel does not request microphone access in this release. Opponent victory-clip recording and playback are disabled in this release.

Push to Unlock

If you enable Push to Unlock, your selected apps, daily goal progress, and app-block events stay on your device and are not uploaded to our servers. On iOS, Apple provides private Screen Time selection tokens rather than revealing the identities of selected apps to PushDuel. On Android, Usage Access lets your device detect when a selected app enters the foreground so it can display the lock screen. PushDuel does not read activity or content inside selected apps.

How we use and share information

We use account and gameplay information to operate accounts, matchmaking, leaderboards, friends, safety features, notifications, and support. Public usernames, profile pictures, levels, rankings, and relevant game statistics are visible to signed-in players where needed for those features. We use:

  • Google Firebase for authentication, database storage, file storage, and backend functions.
  • Google Sign-In when you choose that sign-in method.
  • Apple when you choose Sign in with Apple.
  • Expo push notification services to route push notifications.
  • Firebase Crashlytics for crash and technical diagnostics.
  • Firebase Analytics for app usage and engagement reports.

The current release does not include advertising or subscription-billing SDKs. We do not sell your personal information.

Permissions and your choices

You may decline camera, photo-library, notification, Screen Time, Usage Access, or display-over-apps permission, although the related feature will not work. You can turn off Push to Unlock, remove a profile picture, block players, and report a username or player from inside the app.

Retention and deletion

We retain information while your account is active and as reasonably needed for safety, abuse prevention, legal obligations, and shared match integrity. You can permanently delete your account from the Profile screen. This removes your authentication account, reserved username, profile, private notification tokens, uploaded profile media, friendships, blocks, rate-limit records, and pending requests or challenges. Shared match records and restricted safety, security, or diagnostic records may retain a pseudonymous account identifier where needed so another participant's history remains consistent or abuse can be investigated. They do not retain the deleted public profile or email address.

You can also review account-deletion instructions on our account deletion page.

Age requirement

PushDuel is intended only for people aged 16 or older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 16. If we learn that an account belongs to someone under 16, we will take steps to delete the account and its personal information.

Security

We use access-controlled cloud infrastructure and encrypted network connections to protect information. No transmission or storage method is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your data.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. Updates will be posted here with a revised date. If a future release adds advertising or paid subscriptions, this policy and the relevant store privacy disclosures will be updated before those services are enabled.
