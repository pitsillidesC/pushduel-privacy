PushDuel Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

PushDuel ("we", "our", "the app") is a mobile app for real-time, camera-tracked push-up duels. This page explains what information the app collects, how it's used, and your choices.

Camera use

Camera frames used for live rep tracking are processed entirely on your device and are never uploaded, transmitted, or stored anywhere. The camera feed is used only to detect your body position in real time (via an on-device machine learning model) so the app can count your push-ups. Nothing from this live tracking leaves your phone.

Victory clips

You may optionally record a short (5 second max) video clip of yourself, shown to a matched opponent after a race. Unlike live tracking, this clip is uploaded and stored, since it's something you've explicitly chosen to record and share. Clips are automatically scanned for inappropriate content before they can be shown to anyone, and you can report or block another player's clip at any time from within the app.

Information we collect

  • Account information: email address and display name, via email/password sign-up or Google Sign-In.
  • Profile information: country (optional), gender (optional, used only to offer an accessibility option for push-up form), profile picture (optional).
  • Gameplay data: push-up counts, match results, rankings, streaks, achievements, and friend/challenge activity.
  • Victory clips: if you choose to record one, as described above.
  • Diagnostic data: technical error reports tied to your account, used only to debug problems on real devices.
  • Advertising identifiers: used to show ads (see below).
  • Payment information: if you subscribe to PushDuel Pro, payment is handled entirely by Google Play / our subscription provider — we never see or store your card details.

Who we share information with

We use the following third-party services to run the app. Each only receives the information necessary to perform its function:

  • Google Firebase — account authentication, app data storage, backend infrastructure.
  • Google AdMob — displays ads within the app; may collect an advertising identifier for ad delivery.
  • Google Cloud Video Intelligence — automated scanning of victory clips for inappropriate content before they can be shown to another player.
  • RevenueCat — manages PushDuel Pro subscription billing.

We do not sell your personal information to anyone.

Data deletion

You can request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us at the email below. We will delete your account data within a reasonable time of receiving a verified request.

Children's privacy

PushDuel is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Security

We use industry-standard practices to protect your information, including access-controlled cloud infrastructure and encrypted connections. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we work to protect your data appropriately.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "last updated" date.
